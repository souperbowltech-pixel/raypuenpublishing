## The two things this settles

**1. Where the video codes go.** Each section ends on the page *before* a Teamwork Challenge page,
so the four section ends are **11, 15, 24 and 30**, and the fifth video belongs with the existing QR
on page 32.

Ray originally asked for pages 5, 10, 15 and 19 — only page 15 is right. Page 5 is a Teamwork
Challenge page, and 10 and 19 fall mid-section. He had warned us himself that he might have mixed up
two story versions. **Awaiting his confirmation** (tracker t50).

His captions, on the other hand, match the artwork almost word for word, which is what makes the
mapping above safe to propose:

| Video | His caption, shortened | Page |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | the Goober tries to speak, the forest path | 11 |
| v2 | the Wump's happy purple-ink dance, at the lake | 15 |
| v3 | dive deep with the Wise Sea Turtle, the hidden mountain path | 24 |
| v4 | the finale | 30 |
| v5 | bring two friends (the existing video) | 32 |

**2. Book 1 does not end in victory.** Ray's fourth caption promises the Magic Pen recovered, the
letters flying and a Graduation Ribbon. None of that is in the file: it ends on a cliffhanger, with
the Grumble-Grip holding the pen. Either the video's script changes or the ending pages are missing
from what we hold. Raised with him on 26 September.

## How this page was produced

Rendered with PyMuPDF and read as images, page by page; the text layer only exists on the front
matter, the Teamwork pages and the closing pages, so the story pages were identified from the
artwork itself. To redo it, install `pymupdf` and render the pages of the PDF in
`files from ray/recieved on 24092026/`.
